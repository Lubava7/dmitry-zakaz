BIO

Dima Zimnickiy is a Photographer and Director. Born in 1995 in southwestern Russia to a multicultural family with Russian, Belarusian, and Azerbaijani roots, he discovered photography in 2015, almost by accident. After getting his hands on an old film camera and seeing the results of his first roll, he was immediately hooked. Years of independent practice eventually led him to develop his own visual language.

Originally from a small town, he moved to Moscow, where he lived for eight years. Since 2022, he has been living and working across Asia and beyond. His work is rooted in the spontaneity and uniqueness of the moment — blending street photography and fashion.

He has also been working with video as a Director, DoP, and editor. He has already collaborated with major music artists such as MAYOT, Big Baby Tape, DooMee, UNEEK, KASSETA, and FENDIGLOCK.
